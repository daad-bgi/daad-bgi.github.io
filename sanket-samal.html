About Me

I am a doctoral candidate at the Bayerisches Geoinstitut (BGI), University of Bayreuth, funded by a DAAD scholarship. I completed my master's degree at IISER Pune, where my thesis dealt with the geochemistry of basalts. I then moved from terrestrial basalts and impact glasses to meteorites, to study how the early Solar System's rocky bodies formed and evolved.

Research Interests

  • Petrogenesis of ungrouped and primitive achondrites
  • Isotope geochronology: Al-Mg, Rb-Sr and U-Pb systems
  • Thermal histories and differentiation of asteroidal crusts
  • In-situ trace-element and isotope analysis by LA-ICP-MS

Current Research

My thesis focuses on ungrouped achondrites, particularly the Erg Atouila 001 suite, combining petrography, mineral chemistry and multi-isotope approaches to constrain their origin and cooling history. Part of my lab work is developing in-situ methods for dating and isotope measurement of meteorite minerals.

Analytical Techniques

  • EPMA and SEM-EDS
  • LA-ICP-MS and LA-MC-ICP-MS/MS
  • XRF, Q-ICP-MS and XRD
